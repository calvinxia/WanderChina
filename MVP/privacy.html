Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 31, 2026

WanderChina ("we", "our", "the App") is an AI-powered travel companion for foreign tourists visiting China. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Data We Collect

Account Information — When you register, we collect your email address and a display name you choose. This is used solely to identify your account and save your trips.

Location Data — With your permission, we access your device's GPS to provide city-specific themes, map navigation, route planning, and nearby point-of-interest search. Location data is processed on-device and through our cloud services to deliver these features. We do not store your location history on our servers.

Usage Data — We collect anonymized interaction data (e.g., which features you use, how many itineraries you generate) to improve the App. This data is not linked to your identity.

Crash and Diagnostic Data — We use Sentry to collect crash reports and error logs. These help us identify and fix bugs. Crash data may include device model, OS version, and stack traces but does not include personal content.

Voice Data — When you use the voice translation feature, audio is processed in real time to provide translations. We do not store your voice recordings.

Trip Data — Itineraries you generate and save are stored on our servers (hosted on Tencent Cloud) so you can access them across sessions.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide core App functionality: maps, navigation, itinerary planning, and voice translation
  • To personalize your experience based on your selected destination city
  • To improve App stability and fix bugs
  • To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve features

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. We share data only with the following service providers, strictly for App functionality:

  • Tencent Cloud — Server hosting and database (China region)
  • DeepSeek — AI itinerary generation (receives city and interest preferences, not personal data)
  • Amap (AutoNavi) — Map services and route planning
  • Sentry — Crash reporting and error monitoring

4. Data Retention

Account and trip data are retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Anonymized usage and crash data may be retained for up to 12 months for analytical purposes.

5. Data Security

All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Database access is restricted to our backend cloud functions within a private network (VPC). We follow industry-standard practices to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent for location access at any time through your device settings

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Children's Privacy

WanderChina is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
